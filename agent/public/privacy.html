Privacy at Tilltide

Tilltide is run by Bytez3 (registered office: 128 City Road, London). Contact: info@bytez3.com. Last updated 21 October 2026.

Who this is for

Tilltide is a till and back-office app for independent shops. This page explains what Tilltide keeps about shop owners and staff, and about customers who pay a Tilltide shop.

For a shop's own sales records, the shop decides what happens with them, and Tilltide processes them for the shop. For loyalty codes and receipts, Tilltide is responsible for that information itself.

We don't collect customers' names, email addresses or card numbers. Card and PayPal details stay with PayPal.

Who else sees it

  • PayPal: processes PayPal and card payments and refunds.
  • The Solana blockchain: wallet payments, loyalty points and transfers are recorded publicly onchain. Anyone can see a wallet address and its transactions; this can't be deleted.
  • Anthropic (Claude): answers the shop owner's questions and writes end-of-day notes. It sees sales data, including wallet addresses and PayPal payer ids, for that purpose only.
  • Our hosting providers: Railway (servers and database) and Cloudflare (network security).

We don't sell data or use it for advertising.

Your rights

Under UK GDPR you can ask to see, correct or delete what we hold about you, or object to how we use it. Email info@bytez3.com.

Information recorded on the blockchain can't be deleted by anyone. If you are a customer, the shop you paid may need to keep its sales records for accounting.

You can complain to the Information Commissioner's Office (ico.org.uk).

Cookies

Receipts and these pages use no cookies or tracking. The app stores your sign-in on your device.
