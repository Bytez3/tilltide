# Tilltide: a till that pays you more and keeps your books

*A one-page explainer for shop owners. Print it, or read it out over a coffee.*

**What it is.** An app on a phone that sits on your counter. You tap what they're having (or type a price), and the customer scans a QR code to pay.

**How customers pay**
- **PayPal or card.** Almost everyone can. The money lands in **your** PayPal account.
- **Cash or your old card reader:** tap Cash or Card to record it, so your day's books are complete. Tilltide works out the change.
- **Their phone wallet,** if they use crypto, in PayPal USD (PYUSD) or USDC. It's priced in pounds, and the money lands in your shop's wallet.

**Loyalty without stamp cards.** Every customer earns points automatically, with nothing to carry. Next visit they can spend them: you type the code on their receipt, or their wallet does it for them. You choose how generous to be.

**It does the books for you.** At closing time Tilltide writes your day: takings by how people paid, best sellers, refunds, anything odd. Ask it anything in plain English, like "how did Saturday compare?". Send your bookkeeper a spreadsheet in one tap.

**You stay in charge.** Nothing leaves your accounts unless you approve it on your phone. Staff can use their own phone to take payments, but can't move money or give refunds.

**What it costs:** 0.5% of what you take through Tilltide. Card readers typically charge 1.5–2.5%. No monthly fee during the pilot.

**The pilot**
- 4–6 weeks.
- We set it up with you, on a quiet morning, in about 30 minutes.
- You keep your card reader as a backup.
- We check in weekly, and you can stop any time.
