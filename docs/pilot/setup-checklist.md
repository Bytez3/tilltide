# Pilot setup checklist (first morning, about 30 minutes)

## Before the visit

- [ ] Shop has agreed to the pilot (see `pilot-agreement.md`) and has a PayPal Business account. If not, create one together: about 10 minutes, with ID to hand.
- [ ] Android phone for the counter: Seeker if possible. Tilltide is installed, with a wallet app (Seed Vault on Seeker, or Phantom/Solflare).
- [ ] Owner has a second wallet for their "owner wallet" (where takings are sent). Write down **where they keep the recovery phrase**: never photograph it, never with us.
- [ ] Printed QR stand and the one-pager.

## At the shop

1. **Sign in.** Open Tilltide → shop name → Sign in with wallet. That's the till wallet.
2. **Settings:**
   - prices in GBP
   - wallet payments in PYUSD
   - points per £1 (start with 1)
   - point value (start with £0.01)
   - points can pay up to 50%
   - closing time
   - owner wallet address
3. **PayPal.** Settings → Connect PayPal → finish on PayPal → Check PayPal shows "Connected ✓".
4. **Staff.** Settings → Staff devices → Add → pair each staff phone with the 8-digit code.
5. **Test sale.** Charge £1.00 on PayPal, and you pay it. Then charge £1.00 on Wallet and pay from a test wallet. Check both show Paid ✓ and a receipt.
6. **Test refund.** Refund the £1.00 PayPal sale from Activity and approve with the wallet.
7. **Send takings.** Activity → Send takings to owner → confirm the fee line → sign.
8. **End of day.** Activity → Write today's report → Export CSV, to show what the bookkeeper gets.

## The first week

- [ ] Daily: glance at the end-of-day report together (call or message).
- [ ] Note every question or confusion. That's the product backlog.
- [ ] Count: sales by PayPal and by wallet, points earned and spent, repeat customers, refunds, time saved on the books.

## Rules for in-shop signs

Before putting up any sign that mentions crypto or PYUSD to customers, check the UK rules on promoting cryptoassets. Signs that only say "Pay with PayPal or card" are fine.
