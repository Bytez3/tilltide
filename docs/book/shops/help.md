# Help and troubleshooting

**"No Solana wallet found on this phone."**
Install Phantom or Solflare from the Play Store (or use a Seeker, which has one built in), then try signing in again.

**"Connect your PayPal in Settings to take PayPal payments."**
PayPal sales are off until your PayPal is connected. Go to **Settings → Payments → Connect PayPal**. Meanwhile you can take wallet, cash or card sales.

**A banner says "No connection to Tilltide".**
The phone can't reach the server. Sales already on screen still show as paid once it's back. Use your card reader for new sales, then record them as **Card** sales.

**The customer paid but the till still says waiting.**
Give it a few seconds: PayPal and the Solana network sometimes take a moment. If PayPal is reviewing a payment, the till says so and keeps waiting. Don't take the payment twice.

**A wallet payment says something arrived but it isn't right.**
The customer probably sent too little. Ask them to check their wallet. The sale isn't marked paid until the full amount arrives.

**I typed a cash sale wrong.**
Void it from **Activity** (owner only) and record it again. See [Refunds and mistakes](refunds-and-voids.md).

**Staff can't see the Assistant or Settings.**
That's by design. See [Staff tills](staff.md).
