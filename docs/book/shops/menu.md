# Your menu

Add the things you sell most, so staff can tap them instead of typing prices. 10 to 20 items is plenty. Anything else can still be typed on the keypad.

## Add an item

Go to **Settings → Menu**, type a name and a price, and tap **Add to menu**.

Each item gets an icon and a colour, guessed from its name: "Flat white" gets a coffee cup and "Scone" a cake. To choose a different one, tap the icon next to the name before adding.

## Change or remove an item

Tap an item in the list. You can change its name, price and icon, or remove it.

* **Price changes apply to new sales only.** Past sales keep the price they were sold at, so your reports never change behind your back.
* **Removed items** disappear from the till straight away.

## Who can change the menu

Only the owner. Staff tills can sell from the menu but can't change it, and the server checks every basket against your menu, so a price can't be altered at the till.
