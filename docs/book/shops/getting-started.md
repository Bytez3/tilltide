# Getting started

Setting up takes about 30 minutes. You need an Android phone for the counter, a Solana wallet app on it, and a PayPal Business account.

## What you need

* **A phone for the counter.** Any recent Android phone works. A Solana Seeker is ideal, because its wallet (Seed Vault) is built in.
* **A wallet app on that phone.** Seed Vault on a Seeker, or Phantom or Solflare from the Play Store. This is your *till wallet*: it receives wallet payments, and you use it to approve transfers and refunds. Tilltide never sees its keys.
* **A second wallet for yourself** (your *owner wallet*), where you send takings at the end of the day. Write down where you keep its recovery phrase. Never photograph it.
* **A PayPal Business account.** If you don't have one, it takes about 10 minutes to create with ID to hand.

## Set up the till

1. **Install Tilltide** and open it.
2. **Type your shop's name** and tap **Sign in with wallet**. Your wallet app opens and asks you to sign in. That wallet becomes your till wallet.
3. **Connect PayPal.** Go to **Settings → Payments → Connect PayPal**, finish on PayPal's site, then come back and tap **Check PayPal**. When it says **Connected ✓**, PayPal and card sales go straight to your PayPal account.
4. **Choose your currency** under **Prices in** (pounds by default).
5. **Add your menu.** Go to **Settings → Menu** and add your best sellers with their prices. See [Your menu](menu.md).
6. **Set up loyalty.** Under **Settings → Loyalty**, choose how many points customers earn per £1 and what a point is worth. See [Loyalty points](loyalty.md).
7. **Add your owner wallet and closing time** under **Settings → Takings and reports**.
8. **Pair staff phones** if you have staff. See [Staff tills](staff.md).

The **Getting started** strip on the Charge screen ticks these off as you go, and disappears after your first sale.

## Do a test sale

Before your first customer, run through a sale yourself:

1. Tap an item and charge it on **PayPal**, then pay it from your own phone.
2. Record a £1.00 **Cash** sale and a £1.00 **Card** sale.
3. Check all of them show in **Activity**.
4. Void the test cash sale (see [Refunds and mistakes](refunds-and-voids.md)), and refund the PayPal one.

Keep your card reader as a backup for the first few weeks.
