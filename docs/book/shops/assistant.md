# The assistant

The **Assistant** tab answers questions about your shop in plain English, using your real sales. It's powered by Claude.

## Things to ask

* "What did I take today?"
* "What sold best today?"
* "How does today compare with yesterday?"
* "Who are my regulars?"
* "Refund the cream tea, the scone was cold"
* "Check the till and suggest sending takings"

## What it can and can't do

* **It reads:** your sales, refunds, menu baskets, loyalty points and the till wallet's balance.
* **It prepares:** refunds and transfers to your owner wallet, as cards for you to approve.
* **It can't move money.** Every refund and transfer needs your wallet signature. It can't change settings, prices or the menu.

There's a daily limit on questions per shop, to keep costs fair. If the assistant isn't switched on for your shop yet, everything else (the till, reports and exports) still works.
