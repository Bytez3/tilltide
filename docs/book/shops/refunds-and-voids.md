# Refunds and mistakes

## Refunding a PayPal sale

1. Go to **Activity** and tap **Refund** on the sale. Or ask the assistant: "refund the cream tea, the scone was cold".
2. A refund card appears with the amount. Check it, then tap **Approve with wallet**.
3. Your wallet app shows the exact refund wording. Sign it.
4. Tilltide refunds the customer through PayPal and the card shows **Refunded ✓**.

Signing with your wallet is what proves it's you, so a staff phone or the assistant can never refund on its own. Tilltide also makes sure a refund can't be paid twice, even if the connection drops halfway.

## Wallet sales

Wallet payments land in your till wallet. To refund one, send the tokens back to the customer from your wallet app.

## Cash and card-reader sales

Refund these at the counter as you normally would.

If a cash or card sale was **recorded by mistake** (a typo, or a double tap), the owner can **void** it: in **Activity**, tap **Void** on the sale, then **Tap to confirm**. It's struck through and drops out of takings, reports and exports. If that day's end-of-day report was already written, it's rewritten without the sale.

Only the owner can void, and only cash and card sales. PayPal and wallet sales are refunded instead, because real money moved.
