# End of day and your bookkeeper

## Today at a glance

The top of **Activity** shows today's takings, the number of sales and customers, refunds, and a bar splitting the money by how people paid: PayPal, wallet, cash and card.

## The end-of-day report

At your closing time (set in **Settings → Takings and reports**), Tilltide writes up the day:

* takings and how people paid
* average sale and best sellers
* refunds and net takings
* loyalty points earned and spent
* Tilltide's fees
* the till wallet's balance and anything sent to you
* anything that needs checking, such as a transfer into the till that Tilltide didn't take

With the assistant switched on, a short friendly summary is added on top. Tap **Write report** to write it any time before closing.

## Export for your bookkeeper

Tap **Export CSV** for a spreadsheet of this month so far: every sale, refund and transfer, with date, time, description, price, points discount, money received, how it was paid, and Tilltide's fee. The download link works once, for five minutes.

Add up the **received** column for the money that actually came in. Voided, failed and expired sales have it blank.
