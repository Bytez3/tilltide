# Sending takings to yourself

Wallet payments (PYUSD or USDC) collect in your till wallet. Send them to your own owner wallet whenever you like, for example at the end of the day.

PayPal sales already land in your PayPal account, and cash and card-reader sales never pass through Tilltide, so this is only for wallet takings.

## How

1. Make sure your **owner wallet** is set in **Settings → Takings and reports**.
2. In **Activity**, tap **Send takings to owner**. It suggests everything in the till; change the amount if you want to leave a float.
3. Tap **Review**. The card shows exactly what will happen: how much reaches you, Tilltide's fee (0.5%, shown as an amount), and your owner wallet's address in groups of four so you can check it.
4. Tap **Confirm & sign**. Your wallet app shows the transfer. Sign it.
5. The card shows **Sent ✓** once the transfer is confirmed onchain.

The assistant can suggest a transfer too ("check the till and suggest sending takings"), but it only ever prepares the card. Nothing moves until you sign.

{% hint style="warning" %}
If the card says **Waiting to hear whether the wallet sent it**, don't sign again. Tap **Check again** after a minute. Tilltide won't let the same transfer be signed twice.
{% endhint %}
