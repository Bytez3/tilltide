# Staff tills

Staff can take payments on their own phone without having your wallet or your login.

## Pair a staff phone

1. On your phone: **Settings → Staff devices**, type a name like "Saturday till", and tap **Add staff device**.
2. A pairing code appears (like `1660-2389`). It works once, for 15 minutes.
3. On the staff phone: install Tilltide, tap **I work here: pair this phone as a staff till**, and type the code.

## What staff can and can't do

| Staff can | Only the owner can |
| --- | --- |
| Take PayPal, wallet, cash and card sales | Refund or void sales |
| Sell from the menu | Change the menu, prices or settings |
| See today's takings and sales | Send takings, export, use the assistant |

The server enforces this, not just the app.

## Remove a staff phone

**Settings → Staff devices → Remove**. That phone is signed out straight away.
