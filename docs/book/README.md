# Welcome to Tilltide

Tilltide is a till on a phone for independent shops. It takes PayPal, cards, wallets and cash at the counter, gives every customer loyalty points, and writes up your day at closing time.

* **At the counter:** tap what the customer is having (or type a price), and they scan a QR code to pay with PayPal or a card. Customers with a crypto wallet can pay in PYUSD or USDC. Cash and your existing card reader are recorded on the till too.
* **Loyalty, built in:** every customer earns points automatically and spends them next time. No stamp cards, no app for them to download.
* **An assistant for the back office:** ask "what sold best today?" in plain English. It writes your end-of-day report and can prepare refunds, but nothing moves without your approval.
* **Your money stays yours:** PayPal sales go to your own PayPal account and wallet sales to your shop's wallet. Tilltide never holds your money or your keys.
