# Pricing

No monthly fee during the pilot, and no hardware to buy.

| What | Tilltide's fee | Notes |
| --- | --- | --- |
| PayPal and card sales through Tilltide | 0.5% | Taken by PayPal as a platform fee. PayPal's own fees still apply. |
| Sending wallet takings to yourself | 0.5% | Shown on the confirm card before you sign. Whole pence, rounded down. |
| Wallet sales themselves | Free | The fee is charged when you send the takings to yourself. |
| Cash and card-reader sales you record | Free | That money never passes through Tilltide. |
| The assistant, reports, exports, staff tills | Included | |

For comparison, card readers typically charge 1.5–2.5% per sale.

Tilltide's fees pay for running the service, including the AI behind the assistant.
