# Your data

The full policy is at [/privacy](https://tilltide.bytez3.com/privacy). In short:

* **What's kept:** your shop's settings; each sale (amount, time, how it was paid, the customer's wallet address or PayPal payer id, points); refunds and transfers; end-of-day reports.
* **Why:** to take payments, give loyalty points, write your reports and handle refunds.
* **How long:** while you use Tilltide, then six years for UK accounting records.
* **Customers:** Tilltide never asks for names, emails or phone numbers. Cash and card-reader sales record no customer at all.
* **Your money and keys:** Tilltide never holds either.
* **Leaving:** export your CSV first. Tilltide deletes your data on request, apart from records it must keep by law.
